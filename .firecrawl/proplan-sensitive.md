## Description:

PRO PLAN Adult Sensitive Skin & Stomach Small & Toy Breed Salmon & mMackerel Dry Dog Food is a scientifically developed, dual action formula to help minimise sensitivities.

For dogs with wheat and meat intolerance, it is formulated without wheat, corn, soy, chicken and beef and helps reduce skin inflammation and assist in skin repair, while also maintaining skin moisture and firmness. It contains highly digestible and gentle ingredients for inflamed digestive tract.

These recipes help provide targeted benefits, such as high digestibility and high nutrient absorption to support your dog's long-term health. The small, bite-sized kibble makes chewing easier for small or toy breed dogs.

PRO PLAN Adult Sensitive Skin & Stomach Dry Dog Food is made with high quality salmon and mackerel, perfectly cooked for delicious taste.

Salmon & mackerel (34%); brewers rice; wholegrain sorghum; oats; animal fat (source of omega-6 fatty acids); pea protein; animal digest; minerals, vitamins, amino acids (including calcium, phosphorous, sodium, chloride, potassium, magnesium, zinc, iron, manganese, copper, selenium, iodine, aluminosilicate, vitamin A, B vitamins, vitamin C, vitamin D, vitamin E, choline, folic acid); beet pulp (source of insoluble fibre); fish oil (source of omega-3 fatty acids); chicory (source of inulin, a prebiotic fibre) and natural antioxidants.
