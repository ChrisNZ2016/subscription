## Description:

This protein-rich recipe is packed with 85% quality animal ingredients, including WholePrey ratios of fish, organs, and bone to deliver essential nutrients. Balanced ratios of omega fatty acids support all aspects of your dog’s health – in particular, their skin and coat. With a taste dogs love and the nourishment they need, it’s a diet that supports peak condition and overall vitality. Helping your dog look and feel their best every day.

## Key Benefits:

- All life stages formula: Suitable for puppies, adult and senior dogs.
- BIOLOGICALLY APPROPRIATE: With 38% protein and 85% quality animal ingredients to nourish your dog in line with their evolutionary and biological needs.
- FRESH OR RAW: 2/3 of ORIJEN's meat is FRESH (refrigerated) or RAW (frozen at the peak of freshness) to preserve nutrients.
- WHOLEPREY ANIMAL INGREDIENTS: Featuring WholePrey ratios of meat, organs, cartilage or bone—plus whole fish and cage-free eggs—ORIJEN provides the nutrients your dog needs to thrive.

- NO GRAIN INGREDIENTS: No added grain ingredients, soy, corn, wheat, potato or tapioca.

Suitable for All Life Stages - Excluding Large Breed Puppies
