## Description:

ACANA™ Adult Small Breed is specially crafted to support the lively lifestyle of smaller dogs with a protein-rich recipe that helps maintain a healthy body. This delicious recipe includes 60% quality animal ingredients, like fresh free-run chicken and raw hake, providing the protein needed to support lean muscle and energy. 40% fresh fruits and vegetables, including pumpkin, leafy greens, and apples, deliver fibre and essential nutrients to support digestion, skin, and coat health.

[Learn more](https://petdirect.co.nz/lp/acana-dog) about ACANA Pet Food.

## Key Benefits:

- Fresh, free-run chicken as the 1st ingredient.
- Packed with 60% quality animal ingredients, delivered in WholePrey ratios.
- Smaller kibble size, tailored for small bites.
- Enhanced with freeze-dried chicken and turkey liver, boosting natural flavour and palatability.
- Free from grains, soy, corn, wheat, potatoes, and tapioca.
- No artificial colours, flavours or preservatives.

## Feeding Guidelines:

Your dog is unique and their feeding requirements will vary with their environment, age and activity. We suggest starting with the feeding guide, and then monitor their weight and adjust portions as needed. Split daily ration in half, feed twice per day and provide plenty of fresh water.

|     |     |     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Weight of Dog** | **Active Dog** | **Less Active** |
| **Grams / Day** | **Cups / Day** | **Grams / Day** | **Cups / Day** |
| 2kg | 40g | 1/3c | 30g | 1/4c |
| 5kg | 90g | 3/4c | 60g | 1/2c |
| 7kg | 120g | 1c | 90g | 3/4c |
| 10kg | 160g | 1-1/3c | 120g | 1c |

Use a standard 250ml/120g measuring cup. Serve dry or lightly moistened. See your veterinarian regularly. To help maintain freshness, we recommend sealing the bag tightly and storing it in a cool, dry location.
