#### Original Recipes

Black Hawk Original Adult Dog recipes are complete and balanced foods specially developed for adult dogs, helping them to maintain a healthy body and mind. These recipes use only the best quality Australian ingredients. With premium meats, grains, fresh vegetables, pulses and fruits, as well as delicious and nutritious herbs, vitamins and minerals, we know that every ingredient matters.

With Black Hawk Original Dog Food, you can choose between a variety of flavours including Chicken & Rice, Lamb & Rice, or Fish & Potato, including options for all breed sizes.

#### Grain Free Recipes

Made without wheat, corn, oats, and rice, these grain-free recipes are ideal for dogs with sensitive dietary needs or for owners choosing to exclude grains from their dog’s diets. Black Hawk Grain-Free Dog Food is balance, healthy and nutritious, containing premium ingredients including the highest quality Australian sourced meats, freshest vegetables, pulses and fruits, making sure that your dog gets everything they need and nothing they don’t.

With Black Hawk Grain Free Dog Food, you can choose between a variety of flavours including Salmon, Kangaroo, Chicken or Lamb, including options for all breed sizes.
